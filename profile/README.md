## About AnkiMobile

AnkiMobile is the official iOS companion for spaced repetition study. It helps learners review flashcards on iPhone and iPad, keep memory work steady, and move through decks without losing focus. AnkiMobile flashcards are built for daily use: open a deck, review due cards, rate recall, and let the schedule guide the next session.

AnkiMobile spaced repetition keeps long-term learning practical. Instead of cramming, users review small batches at the right time, so difficult cards return more often and easy cards fade into the background. AnkiMobile iOS review stays readable on smaller screens, with clear buttons, simple navigation, and a layout that supports short sessions during commutes, breaks, or quiet study blocks.

For students, language learners, and professionals preparing for exams, AnkiMobile study sessions fit into real routines. Progress carries across devices when sync is enabled, so a deck started on a computer can continue on a phone. AnkiMobile deck sync keeps cards, notes, and review history aligned, which matters when study time is split between home, school, and work.

AnkiMobile is not a social platform or a game. It is a focused review tool that respects attention. The goal is simple: help users remember what they study through consistent, well-timed repetition.

1. **Open AnkiMobile** – Launch the iOS review environment and confirm your deck list, profile, and sync settings.
2. **Choose a deck** – Select the deck you want to study, or create a new one for a specific subject.
3. **Start a study session** – Begin AnkiMobile study sessions and review the cards that are due.
4. **Rate your recall** – Use the answer buttons to tell AnkiMobile spaced repetition how well you remembered each card.
5. **Sync progress** – Enable AnkiMobile deck sync so review history and deck changes stay consistent across devices.
6. **Review again tomorrow** – Return for the next batch of due cards and keep the schedule moving.
